## 👋 About ProjMitra

ProjMitra is an educational technology platform helping students and aspiring developers learn through practical, real-world technology projects.

Our catalogue features 100 AI, Machine Learning, Data Science and Computer Science project ideas for undergraduate and postgraduate students.

We aim to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical implementation.

## 🎯 Who Is ProjMitra For?

- Final-year engineering students
- B.Tech, BE, BCA and MCA students
- B.Sc. and M.Sc. Computer Science students
- AI and Machine Learning enthusiasts
- Python and Data Science beginners
- Aspiring software developers
